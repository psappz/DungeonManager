# Dire Maul East – Prüfhinweise

Schematischer Vanilla-Entwurf, nicht maßstabsgetreu. Für das spätere Gesamtarchiv aufbewahren.

## Noch zu prüfen

- Dashed passages show known connections with unverified geometry; bends, lengths and compass directions are schematic. Room outlines do not reproduce surveyed footprints.
- The exact Pusillin chase route and intermediate stops are not established; only the starting approach and final combat area are represented.
- The precise ramp junctions and the location of the courtyard jump are unverified. Panels II and III separate the elevated boss platforms from the lower hall.
- The exact Felvine Shard and Isalien summoning positions are unverified; Q4 and Q5 are area markers.
- The final descent and the exit tunnel opened during the Alzzin encounter have unverified geometry. The outgoing arrow marks the exit direction, not a teleport.
- The Eldreth Row entrance and the Crescent Key entrance at Lariss Pavilion are omitted because their exact junctions are not verified for this drawing. Variable A Dusty Tome spawns are not mapped.
