# Stratholme Undead – Prüfhinweise

Schematischer Vanilla-Entwurf für einen Lauf ab Service Gate. Für das Gesamtarchiv aufbewahren.

## Offene Punkte

- Dashed routes express known connections, not verified street geometry. Room shapes, distances and relative elevations are schematic.
- Stonespine patrol and the late Barthilas position are approximate. The alternative early Barthilas location and the main-gate approach are omitted.
- The exact street junctions between the three ziggurats are unverified; panel II is a connectivity abstraction, not a literal central crossroads.
- The Black Guard Swordsmith plans, Aurius, Scourge Data, ritual symbol and Ysida positions are approximate area markers.
- The cross-wing connection to Stratholme Live is not mapped until a shared street layout is verified. Variable crates and roaming quest targets are omitted.
