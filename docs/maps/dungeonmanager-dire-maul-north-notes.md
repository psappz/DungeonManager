# Dire Maul North – Prüfhinweise

Schematischer Vanilla-Entwurf; diese Datei gehört ins Gesamtarchiv.

## Offene Punkte

- Dashed corridors indicate documented connections with unverified bends, dimensions and orientation. Room outlines are schematic abstractions, not surveyed footprints.
- The patrol paths of Mol'dar, Fengus and Slip'kik are not established; markers 1, 3 and 4 indicate search areas.
- Exact positions of the trap, Knot, the tannin basket and the Kromcrush quest interaction are unverified; Q2-Q5 are area markers.
- The detailed route from the upper platform to Kromcrush, including any intermediate landings, is unverified and is compressed into a dashed connection.
- Both chest positions are schematic. The Athenaeum side connection is omitted until its junction and descending passage can be verified together with Dire Maul West.
- No safe path around hostile packs or patrols is asserted. Tribute uses the same rooms; it is not a separate physical corridor.

## Tributroute

Die Bossnummern beschreiben einen vollständigen Besuch. Für einen vollständigen Tributlauf werden die übrigen Bosse verschont; Falle und Ogerverkleidung helfen dabei. Die Karte garantiert keinen sicheren Laufweg zwischen Gegnergruppen.
